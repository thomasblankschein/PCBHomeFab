Exposure series

A test exposure to find the right exposure time. The panel is divided into strips with the same test pattern (tracks and gaps from 0.1 to 0.5 mm, pads, SMD rows, a copper pour, small text). The panel stays on the display; every exposure lights one strip more, so the first strip gets the longest time and the last one the shortest.

Settings

Calibration

Values of your printer profile. Change them only after measuring a test exposure.

How to use it

  1. Lay a photoresist board of the panel size on the display with the copper side down, as for a normal exposure.
  2. Start the file from the USB stick. The printer exposes all layers by itself; do not move the board. Between the layers it lifts the Z axis for a few seconds.
  3. Develop and etch the board as usual.
  4. Choose the shortest time at which the exposed areas are etched clean and the fine tracks and gaps are still sharp. Every strip shows its time; seen from the copper side the first strip (longest time) is on the left.
  5. Enter this time as the exposure time in the settings of the editor.

Black areas of the display let a little UV light through. In a long series the strips with short times get a small extra dose.
